## Business Problem Summary

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales together with current stock levels. She needs clear answers on overall performance, whether the business is growing, which products require urgent restocking, and where she should focus her efforts to improve results.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Week-on-week growth of 10.66%** – Week 2 total sales (KES 24,400) exceeded Week 1 (KES 22,050), showing positive momentum.
2. **Weekends drive the business** – Average weekend sales (KES 4,900) are 82.5% higher than weekday sales (KES 2,685).
3. **Immediate restock needed for Tomatoes and Sukuma** – Both items are already at or below their reorder levels and risk stock-outs on high-demand days.
